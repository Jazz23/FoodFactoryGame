## What the new tests show

- Domain (`FarmOrderTests`): farm types cycle in lot order and cover all 11 raw ingredients; a delivery charges goods and fee
  once (a retry replays), its supplier truck carries refrigerated goods from the gate, unloads 10 units and leaves the world
  with no refund; a pickup sends the parked truck, refuses reassignment (`on-order`) and a second order (`truck-busy`), loads at
  the gate and parks at the shop; owning the farm halves the goods price; a full dock times out and refunds goods and fee; nine
  rejections leave the state byte-identical; a recurring order runs at the next game hour and stops when cancelled; an open
  order survives a save and reload, and a v25 payload upgrades; site renames are checked.
- WorldGen (`PhoneSessionTests`, seed `phone`): the phone opens wider than 60% of the screen, draws the generated layout, is
  above the HUD in the panel, disables walking and gives it back on close; buying the nearest dairy farm from the map and
  ordering two packs of milk charges half price plus the fee and a supplier truck delivers 10 milk to the starting dock's
  Incoming at a raised test clock rate; the Trucks app holds the logistics screen and buys a truck there, and gives the screen
  back on other apps and on close; renaming the starting restaurant and hiring work from the phone, Tasks opens the employee
  screen and Esc there returns to the phone.
