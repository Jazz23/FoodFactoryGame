# Verification: factory floors and the freight elevator (2026-09-24)

Decision: [0020](../decisions/0020-factory-floors-and-elevator.md).

Compilation: live Editor `recompile` after each change, no errors; the only warnings were existing `CS0618` warnings
(`FindObjectsSortMode` in PlayMode tests, FishNet's Mirror upgrade menu). Scene wiring was applied to the open `DevSite`
through the Editor (`SerializedObject`) and saved; `AgentScripts/BuildDevSite.cs` was updated to author the same wiring but
was **not re-run**. The saved `DevSite` also gained a `UniversalAdditionalLightData` component on its light, added by URP when
the scene was saved; it is unrelated to this feature.

Before choosing the dev factory's cells, a copy of the owner's application save was loaded in a temporary folder (the
original was not opened for writing). A first choice, 8x6 at (0, 14), was `blocked` there by a placed oven at (5, 17), so the
factory moved to 5x10 at (15, 10), which is clear in that save, in fresh dev worlds and in every PlayMode test.
`EnsureFactory` will add it there on the next server start; the save upgrades from v7 to v8 in memory and is rewritten at the
next commit.

First-run failures: the three `DevWorldBuildingTests` assumed the dev site has exactly one building (`Single()`, an
expected list of one ID, and an empty list when the restaurant is blocked). The seed now intentionally adds the factory, so
the tests were updated to expect both shells and to check that a blocked shell is skipped while the other is still added.

New or changed coverage:

- `FloorTests` (Goods, 5, new):
  - an order charges once, fixes the elevator, replays on retry, later floors reuse the shaft, and the durable path commits
    floor and payment together;
  - rejections (`not-a-factory`, `forbidden`, `invalid-elevator` on a wall or door, `blocked`, `insufficient-funds`,
    `max-floors`, `no-floor-offer`) leave the snapshot unchanged;
  - upper-floor placement needs a floor and the interior, never on the elevator on any storey; levels do not block each
    other; recovery rejects a piece above the top floor, an elevator outside the interior and a multi-storey restaurant;
    pickup resets the level;
  - a ground belt pointing at a cell with only an upper-floor belt stops its item at its own end;
  - a v7 row loads as one-storey restaurants.
- `DevWorldBuildingTests` (Session, 4; 3 changed, 1 new): both shells in a new world, clear of the seed and the spawn points;
  an older save gains both once; a blocked restaurant or a blocked factory is skipped alone.
- `SessionAuthoringTests` (2 changed): `DevSite` has one `ElevatorRider` with its presenter and actions, and the equipment
  presenter, belt presenter and interaction reference the `BuildingPresenter`; `FloorUp`/`FloorDown` bind PgUp/PgDn as
  presses.
- `BuildingPresenterTests` (PlayMode, 1 new, 1 changed): in a real DevSite host, the inventory screen's Build button adds a
  floor with the elevator on the avatar's cell and charges the price; the new storey is hidden from below; riding up puts the
  avatar on level 1, where it stays for 30 frames on the slab; no ride above the top; an oven placed upstairs sits 3 m up
  and is hidden after riding down. The existing test now finds the restaurant by ID.

Running-game observations: in `ground.png` the factory (centre) shows the ground-floor oven and the avatar on the elevator
cell; nothing of the upper storey is drawn. In `upper.png` the slab covers the interior, the upstairs oven stands near the
south wall, and the ground-floor oven is not visible. The dark strip on the right was not identified; it is most likely the roof of the neighbouring restaurant.

Not verified: a player build; a two-process session (a remote client seeing another player hidden on a storey); pressing
PgUp/PgDn through the Input System (the tests call `ElevatorRider.Ride`); clicking the Build button with the pointer (the
test calls `ClickAddFloor`, the button's handler); independent visual review; re-running `BuildDevSite`.
