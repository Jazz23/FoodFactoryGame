## Follow-up: creating a world from the panel (2026-09-27)

The owner reported that Host created no world. Cause (checked read-only): the default save
`LocalLow/DefaultCompany/FoodFactoryGame/Saves/dev-world/world.db` already held a DevSite world, so Host loaded it and, by
design, it has no layout. Second cause: the city started about 475 m away, past the player camera's 1,000 m far plane for
most of the map. Fixes: WorldGen saves under `Saves/worldgen`, the panel gained World and New world, the city now starts
40 m north of the dev site, and local cameras draw to about 2.4 km while a layout is shown.

Test through the panel's own buttons in play mode (the saves root pointed at the session scratch directory first; the
panel logic after that was unmodified): the scene's default save resolved to `Saves/worldgen`; **Host** with a blank seed
created `worldgen` (random seed 11495844360425057091, 1,015 buildings, readout line shown, player camera far plane 2,400);
**Host** again with a different seed reopened it unchanged; **New world** with "Sunny Valley" created `world-1` (1,024
buildings, client hash bf9d791d…7657). No console errors or warnings. Captures:
[player view after Host](worldgen-20260927/worldgen-player-view-after-host-1.png) (default pitch looks down at the avatar)
and [from spawn looking north](worldgen-20260927/worldgen-from-spawn-looking-north.png). EditMode World + Session + Baseline:
job `19781525c0da4a9fa00808091c34830c`, 106 matched, 106 passed.
