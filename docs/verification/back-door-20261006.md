### Failures met on the way and their causes

- `RestaurantFurnishingTests.RestaurantDocksNeedAStreetPathAndServeOneTruckAtATime` placed docks with no back door (the 0034 rule).
  Rewritten as `RestaurantDocksServeOneTruckAtATime` with a back door; the rule cases moved to `BackDoorTests`.
- `DevWorldBuildingTests` (2): the dev restaurant's door list now has the back door, and the "something on the walls" fridge stood
  at (9, 4), the new back door's opening. The fridge now stands at (9, 3), still on the wall.
- `BuildingPresenterTests.WalkingInSwitchesToTopDown...` probed the west wall at z = 4, now the back door. It probes z = 6 and also
  checks the back door is an opening.
- `RestaurantBuildingSessionTests.BuildModeOrdersReachTheServerAndATeammate` timed out removing an outer wall. Cause: with the shell
  open (no interior) the first doorstep rule found two candidate cells, so the back door lost its doorstep and the order was
  refused. Fixed in code (`SiteGrid.Doorstep` prefers the side outside the footprint), covered by `BackDoorTests`; the test was not
  changed.
- `WorldGenSessionTests` (2): the start kit now includes `start-dock`, and the apron dock test picks a cell the new rule accepts.
