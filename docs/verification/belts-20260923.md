## Coverage

- Domain (`BeltTests`, test-only 8×6 site): placing consumes one belt item and dragging over a belt only turns it (same ID, no cost); `out-of-bounds`, `blocked` by equipment, `invalid-rotation`, `forbidden`, `no-belts` change nothing, and belts block equipment in turn. Shapes and links: a one-side feeder curves the belt and enters at 0; a feeder behind straightens it and the side belt side-loads at the middle; both sides straight; the curve flips to the other side; head-on belts never link. Items: exactly one unit leaves the inventory, travels one tile per second, and six items queue at the end at 210/150/90/30 and 210/150 on the belt before it (spacing across the boundary); a side-loaded item enters at the middle; one 7 s step equals seven 1 s steps; a four-belt loop keeps every item moving. `Transfer` into or out of a belt is `invalid-route`; the fourth centre placement is `belt-full`. Taking an item off: `forbidden` for an ungranted player or unknown lot, `not-on-belt` for an inventory lot, a failed commit leaves it riding, any granted player may take it (same ID, position reset, replayable, saved), a taken dough tops up a full inventory's dough slot, and a new stack into a full inventory is `capacity` with nothing changed. Removal with a full inventory is `capacity` and changes nothing; removal returns the belt and riding items with their IDs. Failed commits roll placement and removal back; belts and a riding item's position survive save and load; a v3 save loads as v4; `Validate` rejects belts on equipment, out of bounds, bad directions, duplicates, belt locations without belts, stacked riding lots and positions off the belt.
- Existing schema tests (v1, v2) now expect the current schema v4.
- Authoring: DevSite has one `BeltPresenter` with all prefabs and materials and no belt instances; the interaction has `placeItemAction` and `belts`; items are dough 20, bread 20, belt 100, each with an icon; `Player/PlaceItem` is bound to Z and `Player/TakeItem` to F, both presses; the interaction has `takeItemAction`. Each belt prefab has one trigger box, its belt surface spans exactly (−0.5, 0.8, −0.5)–(0.5, 0.8, 0.5), and every material slot is a project belt material with the tread texture on the surface.
- PlayMode `BeltPlacementTests.DragWithRotateLaysACornerAndItemsRideToTheEnd` (real `DevSite` host, temp paths, virtual Input System mouse and keyboard, crosshair aimed by moving the avatar under a steep camera): the dev seed has 200 storage belts and 50 starter belts; the belt stack stays on the cursor after the inventory closes, with its icon shown; a hover ghost shows; press, drag north three cells, R with the crosshair to the east, drag east four cells, release: eight belts, (2,2..4) north and (2..6,5) east, 42 belts left (the head turn was free), the head is `CurveFromRight` and drawn with the right corner prefab, seven straights. Every belt surface uses the one scrolling tread material and its offset changes. R with an empty cursor turns a belt (four times, back to east). Dough carried out the same way shows its ghost at the belt's middle; Z puts exactly one on the belt; it rides seven belts to rest at the end and is drawn there. F with an empty cursor takes it back off (same ID, inventory dough back to 5, visual gone). Another dough is put on the last belt with Z; holding right click removes that belt; the belt and the dough come back; the save holds seven belts.
