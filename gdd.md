# Superseded Design Notes

Historical reference only. [Food_Factory_Restaurant_GDD.md](Food_Factory_Restaurant_GDD.md) is the authoritative gameplay design. Do not implement these notes as current requirements: they include superseded food quality, equipment manufacturing, construction, and camera ideas.

---

- Manufacturing plant \= logistics (trucks/trains) to transport food to restaurants.  
  - If you transport raw ingredients instead, you can make better food in the kitchen.  
- The kitchen requires space, so does the rest of the restaurant. More tables mean more customers, bigger kitchens mean more food or unlock bigger recipes.  
- Competing restaurants in the area can be bought out if they are performing poorly.  
  - Competing restaurants steal customers if they have higher quality food or the type of food that area likes.  
- Different areas of the map have different tastes in food and also are richer/poorer and might require higher quality food.  
- Certain foods may spoil if not delivered to the kitchen/refrigerators in time.  
- Farms are also purchasable across the map.  
- You can also make a factory to produce things like refrigerators, pavement for new roads, train stations/trains, etc.  
- Employees as constructions bots that can do anything a character can do.  
  - They can be remotely instructed.  
  - They can be transported.  
  - They can fulfill any role (chef, waiter, factory worker)  
- You are a physical character that walks around.  
- Procedural map.  
- Inside a building is top down, outside is isometric.  
- All machines are unlocked immediately  
- Machines cost money
